Beta tester guide

Put TravelFox on your home screen

TravelFox isn't in the app stores yet. You install it straight from the web instead. It takes under a minute, needs no download from a store, and then opens full-screen from its own icon like any other app.

Open this on your phone
app.thecitytrail.com
Reading this on a computer? Type the address into your phone's browser, or send it to yourself.

Android, using Chrome

Use Chrome. It's the browser most Android phones come with. Samsung Internet also works; see the note under the steps.

  1. Open the address in Chrome

    Go to app.thecitytrail.com and wait for TravelFox to finish loading.

  2. Open Chrome's menu

    Tap the three dots at the top right of the screen.

  3. Choose “Install app”

    Tap Install app. On some phones it's called Add to home screen instead. Chrome may also show an install bar at the bottom of the page by itself; tapping that does the same thing.

  4. Confirm

    Tap Install. The orange fox icon appears on your home screen or in your app drawer after a few seconds.

  5. Open it from the icon

    From now on, open TravelFox from its icon rather than from Chrome. It runs full-screen, without the address bar.

Using Samsung Internet?

Tap the ☰ menu at the bottom right, then Add page to, then Home screen. Some versions show a download-arrow icon in the address bar that installs it in one tap.

iPhone, using Safari

Use Safari if you can. Chrome on iPhone can also do it with the same Share steps, but Safari is the most reliable.

  1. Open the address in Safari

    Go to app.thecitytrail.com and wait for TravelFox to finish loading.

  2. Tap the Share button

    Tap Share, the square with an arrow pointing up. On recent iOS versions you first tap ••• at the bottom of the screen, then Share.

  3. Choose “Add to Home Screen”

    Scroll down the list that slides up and tap Add to Home Screen. If you don't see it, tap View More or Edit Actions at the bottom of the list.

  4. Keep “Open as Web App” on, then Add

    If you see an Open as Web App switch, leave it on. Check the name says TravelFox and tap Add at the top right.

  5. Open it from the icon

    The fox icon is now on your home screen. Always open TravelFox from there, not from Safari, so it runs full-screen like an app.

Good to know

How do I get updates?

You don't need to do anything. Each time you open TravelFox it checks for the latest version. If something looks out of date, close the app fully and open it again.

The first load is slow

During the beta our server goes to sleep when nobody is using it. The first time you open the app after a quiet spell it can take up to a minute to wake up. After that it's quick.

I can't find the install option

Make sure you're in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone), not inside another app's built-in browser. Links opened from WhatsApp, Instagram or email often open in a mini-browser. Use its menu to choose “Open in Chrome” or “Open in Safari” first.

How do I remove it?

Treat it like any other app. Press and hold the TravelFox icon, then choose Uninstall (Android) or Remove App, then Delete (iPhone).
